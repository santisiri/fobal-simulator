### What the extra bytes bought

| | v1 | v2 | delta |

The brief's test was "a 3 KB atlas that looks dramatically better is a good
trade; 15 KB of invisible microvariation is not." This spends
**184 bytes** — 11% — and buys five new classes
(per-head shading, ears, necks, builds, collars) plus per-head facial
architecture. The architecture itself is FREE: parts are authored once in
local space and translated onto the chosen head's anchors, so six skulls give
six faces from one stored copy of each feature.

It also spends bytes DOWNWARD where they were not earning: eyes 10 to
6, brows 8 to 6, mouths 10 to 7,
headwear 10 to 6, hair 24 to 21. The hair cut was not taste:
`tools/silhouette-lib.mjs` strips colour and measures every pair of masks, and
Cornrows and Undercut were **pixel-identical**.

## 2 · The anchor system (item 20)

Stored per head — four integers, 24 bytes total:

| head | width | chinY | eyeY | eyeGap |
|---|--:|--:|--:|--:|
| Oval | 14 | 21 | 12 | 3 |
| Long | 12 | 22 | 12 | 2 |
| Round | 15 | 20 | 12 | 4 |
| Square | 16 | 21 | 12 | 5 |
| Heavy Jaw | 16 | 22 | 11 | 5 |
| Tapered | 15 | 21 | 11 | 4 |

Derived on BOTH sides, never stored (`spec/anchors.js` and
`contracts/src/art/FobalAnchors.sol` compute these identically):

```
headX      = 16 - (w >> 1)          browY      = eyeY - 3
noseX      = 16   (faces are centred)  noseY      = eyeY + 2
leftEyeX   = 16 - (gap >> 1) - 4     mouthY     = chinY - 3
rightEyeX  = 16 + (gap >> 1) + (gap & 1)
earY       = eyeY                    earLeftX   = headX - 3
earRightX  = headX + w - 1           widthClass = w<=12 ? 0 : w<=14 ? 1 : 2
```

`mouthY = chinY - 3` is derived rather than authored on purpose: pinning the
mouth a fixed distance above the chin is what lets ONE authored beard clear
the mouth on all six skulls.

## 3 · The compatibility matrix (item 16)

Deterministic and STABLE: no rule rerolls a lane. A restricted trait maps to a
defined alternative, so the same seed on a different head lands somewhere
predictable rather than in a different draw.

### Head width restricts mouth width

| width class | heads | eligible mouths |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Long | Neutral (4px), Open (4px), Compressed (3px) |
| 1 | Oval | Neutral (4px), Slight (5px), Open (4px), Downturned (5px), Compressed (3px) |
| 2 | Round, Square, Heavy Jaw, Tapered | Neutral (4px), Stern (6px), Slight (5px), Wide Smile (6px), Open (4px), Downturned (5px), Compressed (3px) |

The lane picks WITHIN the eligible set, so every mouth stays reachable on the
heads that can carry it.

### Build decides neck

| build | Slim | Normal | Broad | Very Broad |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| neck | Narrow | Normal | Normal | Thick |

Derived, not drawn from its own lane — a slim player with a heavyweight's
throat reads as a bug, and this costs one fewer keccak.

### Hair under headwear

Headwear tagged `covers` maps hair through a fallback that is keyed by NAME
and idempotent (applying it twice equals applying it once):

| hair | becomes |
|---|---|
| Afro | Curly |
| High Top | Buzz |
| Flat Top | Buzz |
| Mohawk | Buzz |
| Dreads | Braids |
| Long | Side Part |
| Ponytail | Topknot |
| Wavy | Short |

Everything else is a fixed point and passes through unchanged.

### The remaining rules

| condition | resolution | why |
|---|---|---|
| bald + a band-tagged headwear | headwear becomes None | nothing to hold it on |
| full or long beard + Open mouth | mouth becomes Neutral | an open mouth inside a beard reads as a hole. Neutral, **not** Stern: Stern is 6px and illegal on a narrow skull, which is how a constraint pass quietly undoes the rule above it |
| full or long beard + Polo collar | collar becomes Crew | the beard covers the collar points |
| protruding ears + a covering headwear | ears become Normal | the hat would clip through them |
